Healthy hair basics: six chapters 📚

A friendly read, about 8 minutes.

01

Start with the scalp 🌱

Healthy hair grows from a healthy scalp. Massage gently with your fingertips while washing to lift oil and dirt. Avoid scratching with nails.

02

Wash smart 🚿

Use lukewarm water and focus shampoo on the scalp, not the lengths. How often you wash depends on your hair type, oil level and lifestyle.

03

Condition and detangle 🧴

Apply conditioner to the mid-lengths and ends. Detangle gently from the ends upward with a wide-tooth comb to reduce breakage.

04

Be kind with heat 🔥

Heat styling can dry hair out. Use a heat protectant, choose lower temperatures and give your hair rest days.

05

Trim regularly ✂️

Trimming split ends every two to three months is a common guideline. It keeps hair looking neat and prevents splits from travelling upward.

06

Eat, drink and rest 🥗

Hair reflects overall health. Balanced meals with protein, iron and vitamins, enough water and good sleep all help. A smooth pillowcase can reduce friction at night.

Myths and facts 🔍

Tap a myth to read the facts.

Myth: Cutting hair makes it grow faster

Hair grows from the scalp, so trimming does not change the speed. It only removes damaged ends, which helps hair look healthier and thicker.

Myth: Washing every day damages hair

It depends on your hair and scalp. A gentle shampoo used sensibly is fine for many people, especially with oily scalps.

Myth: Brushing 100 times makes hair shiny

Over-brushing can cause breakage. Brush gently and only as much as needed to detangle and style.

Myth: Expensive products are always better

What matters is whether a product suits your hair type and needs. Read the ingredient list and choose what works for you.
